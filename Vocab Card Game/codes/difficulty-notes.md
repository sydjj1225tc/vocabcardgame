# Revised multi-definition power points

The user’s rule supersedes the earlier single-sense rubric: every multi-definition word must score above 100, within the 10–150 range. These are provisional instructional judgments, not Oxford ratings or standardized ninth-grade measures.

| Word | Points | Coverage | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| value | 150 | Five noun senses and two verb senses | Seven meanings across everyday, ethical, financial and mathematical uses; two parts of speech |
| obtain | 130 | Two verb senses | Formal acquisition sense plus the much less familiar “exist” sense for rules, systems and customs |
| differ | 110 | Two verb senses | Related ideas of being different and disagreeing, supported by the familiar word different |

## Sources and wording

All numbered headword senses were checked September 22, 2026:

- https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/value_1 — noun senses 1–5
- https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/value_2 — verb senses 1–2, numbered 6–7 on the combined card
- https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/obtain — verb senses 1–2
- https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/differ — verb senses 1–2

Value uses concise paraphrases of all seven meanings. Obtain retains the first definition and paraphrases the second with its grammatical context. Differ spells out “or” instead of dictionary slash notation. Definitions do not end in periods. Example sentences, idioms, synonyms, and separately linked compound entries are not additional numbered headword senses.

The value illustration supports usefulness and monetary worth, not every abstract sense individually. The image is a memory cue, not a replacement for learning each meaning. The value reading panel is enlarged to accommodate seven numbered meanings.

The human boy was removed from the value image. Keep the guardian as the sole character unless another character is necessary to communicate the meaning.
